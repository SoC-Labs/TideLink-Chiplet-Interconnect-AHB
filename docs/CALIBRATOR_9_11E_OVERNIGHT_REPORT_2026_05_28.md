## §9.11e Layer-2 design

**Hypothesis (from independent Agent 2 + 3 + OVERNIGHT analysis):** the
`{P, P}` (same-byte-repeated) training pattern lacks byte-boundary
transitions when `P[7] == P[0]` (true for several of our per-lane
patterns). Real data has random byte-to-byte transitions, exercising a
narrower deserialiser eye than training does. Calibrator picks a
(slip, phase) that's correct for training's predictable transition
density but wrong for real-data's full transition spectrum.

**Fix:** emit `{P, ~P}` (byte concatenated with its complement) during
training_mode. The complement at the byte boundary forces an 8-bit
transition every 16-bit word (`~P[7] → P[0]` always flips), exercising
the high-ISI conditions real data has. Calibrator is forced to find a
(slip, phase) robust under byte-boundary transitions, not just
same-byte-repeat eye.
